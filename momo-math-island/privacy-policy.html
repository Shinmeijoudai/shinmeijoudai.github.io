About Momo Math Island

Momo Math Island is an educational mathematics game created by Momo Kids Lab for children and families. Momo Math Island is designed to make learning mathematics fun and engaging by combining basic math practice with interactive gameplay. The Game helps children practice and develop their counting, comparing, addition, and subtraction skills while having fun and progressing through the game. Through fun questions and activities, children can practice counting, comparing numbers, addition, and subtraction.

Our goal is to give children a fun way to practice mathematics, build confidence with numbers, improve their basic math skills, and enjoy learning through play.

Momo Math Island is designed primarily as an offline game and is offered as a one-time purchase.

Privacy

Momo Kids Lab respects the privacy of children, parents, guardians, and all players.

Momo Math Island is designed to operate without requiring players to provide personal information. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through normal gameplay.

The Game does not require players to provide their full name, email address, telephone number, home address, precise location, photographs, videos, voice recordings, contacts, messages, passwords, social media information, or personal profiles in order to play the Game.

We do not sell or rent players' personal information to third parties.

Offline Gameplay

Momo Math Island is designed to be played offline, and an internet connection is not required for normal gameplay.

Game progress, stars, collected stickers and rewards, settings, and other game-related information may be stored locally on the player's device so that the Game can save and remember gameplay progress.

Locally stored gameplay information is not intentionally transmitted to Momo Kids Lab.

Deleting the Game, clearing application data, changing devices, resetting a device, or experiencing a device failure may result in the loss of locally stored game progress. Momo Kids Lab is not responsible for the loss of locally stored game data caused by deletion, device failure, operating-system changes, device replacement, or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

One-Time Purchase

Momo Math Island is intended to be a one-time purchase game. Players are not required to pay recurring fees to continue playing the Game.

The Game does not require subscriptions, recurring payments, gacha purchases, loot boxes, real-money virtual currency, pay-to-win purchases, or third-party advertising.

Any purchase required to obtain the Game is processed through the applicable official app store, such as Google Play.

Momo Kids Lab does not receive or store a player's credit-card or payment-card information through the Game. Payment processing is handled by the applicable platform according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Advertising

Momo Math Island does not display third-party advertisements.

We do not use the Game to create advertising profiles for children, and we do not sell or rent children's personal information to advertisers.
