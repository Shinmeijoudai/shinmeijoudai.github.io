One island, four ways to play with numbers

Rewards

Stars, outfits and island decorations

Every finished round earns stars. Stars unlock outfits for Momo and Beep, plus rainbows and flower gardens to decorate the island. Everything is earned by playing — nothing is ever sold.

Parent FAQ

Does the game collect any data?

No. There are no accounts and no tracking. Progress is saved only on your device.

Are there in-app purchases?

No. One purchase gets the full game, with every reward earned through play.

What happens when my child gets an answer wrong?

Nothing is lost. Momo encourages them to try again and can offer a gentle hint.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or newer.
