More adventures are on the way

Momo is packing her bag for new learning worlds. Every game shares the same safe, kind rules.

How kids learn

Play first. Learning follows.

Short, happy rounds

Bite-sized activities fit little attention spans and end on a win.

Mistakes are welcome

Wrong taps cost nothing. Momo encourages another try and offers a hint.

Rewards that feel earned

Stars, outfits and room decorations — never coins to buy.

Calm by design

Soft music, gentle colors and no timers racing the clock.

Meet Momo

A brave little explorer who never gives up

Momo wears her favorite fox-ear hat on every adventure. She cheers when kids get it right, offers a hint when they're stuck, and always says "Let's try again!"

Her blue buddy is always by her side — and both of them love new outfits. Kids earn crowns, bows, stars and headphones as they play.

Try it: tap an outfit to dress them up.
