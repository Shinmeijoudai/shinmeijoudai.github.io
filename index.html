About Momo Kids Lab

Momo Kids Lab creates educational games for children and families, where kids play and learn basic skills at the same time. Each game is an adventure with Momo, our friendly main character, and focuses on a different kind of learning, from numbers and math to puzzles, problem-solving and more.

Our games are made to be:

  • Fun first: learning happens through play, with no pressure.
  • Kid-safe: no third-party ads and no personal information needed to play.
  • Offline friendly: designed to be played without an internet connection.
  • One-time purchase: no subscriptions or pay-to-win purchases.

Momo Puzzle World

A gentle puzzle adventure for young children. Momo, a cheerful explorer in a fox-ear hat, and her blue buddy Bibi travel through worlds solving puzzles together. Momo cheers every right answer, says "let's try again" after a wrong tap, and points the way when a hint is needed. Wrong taps never cost anything.

  • Adventure: 40 levels across Momo's Forest and Ocean World.
  • Quick Puzzle: 100 levels of mixed puzzles that grow harder as you go.
  • Daily Puzzle: a new puzzle every day, with a streak that never punishes missed days.
  • Puzzle Book: harder replays of finished levels.
  • Rewards: unlock hats for Momo's Closet and furniture for My Room.

Builds eight thinking skills: observation, matching, rotation, logic, patterns, spatial sense, memory and problem solving. Plays offline in English, Spanish and Thai, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.
