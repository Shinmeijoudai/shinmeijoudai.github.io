Meet Momo

A brave little explorer who never gives up

Momo wears her favorite fox-ear hat on every adventure. She cheers when kids get it right, offers a hint when they're stuck, and always says "Let's try again!"

Her blue buddy is always by her side — and both of them love new outfits. Kids earn crowns, bows, stars and headphones as they play.

Try it: tap an outfit to dress them up.
