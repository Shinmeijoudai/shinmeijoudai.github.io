About Momo Kids Lab

Momo Kids Lab creates educational games for children and families, where kids play and learn basic skills at the same time. Each game is an adventure with Momo, our friendly main character, and focuses on a different kind of learning, from numbers and math to puzzles, problem-solving and more.

Our games are made to be:

  • Fun first: learning happens through play, with no pressure.
  • Kid-safe: no third-party ads and no personal information needed to play.
  • Offline friendly: designed to be played without an internet connection.
  • One-time purchase: no subscriptions or pay-to-win purchases.

Momo Puzzle World

Join Momo, a cheerful explorer in a fox-ear hat, and her round blue buddy Bibi on a gentle puzzle adventure through five magical worlds: the Forest, the Ocean, Candy World, Dinosaur World and Space. Every puzzle your child solves helps Momo on her journey, and Momo cheers every success along the way.

What your child learns

Every puzzle practices a real thinking skill:

  • Looking closely: spotting small details and finding hidden things
  • Matching: seeing what is the same and what is different
  • Patterns: guessing what comes next, an early step toward math
  • Memory: remembering where things are and in what order
  • Shapes and space: turning pieces and finding the right path
  • Logic and problem solving: thinking ahead and trying a plan

Puzzles start very easy and grow slowly with your child, so every child can succeed.

Growing confidence and kindness

  • No failing: a wrong tap just gets a soft "Let's try again!", and nothing is ever lost.
  • No timers: every child plays at their own pace.
  • Helpful hints: Momo points the way in small steps, so your child still solves it themselves.
  • Caring for a friend: children solve puzzles to help Momo, which builds kindness and empathy.
  • Creativity: children dress Momo up and decorate her room their own way.

Lots to play

  • Adventure: 100 levels across five worlds, each with its own story.
  • Quick Puzzle: 100 mixed puzzles that get a little harder as you go.
  • Daily Puzzle: a new puzzle every day. Your streak is never reset for missed days.
  • Puzzle Book: replay finished levels as harder challenges.
  • Collection: gather special puzzle pieces from every world.
  • Closet: unlock hats and outfits, like the Explorer look and the Little Chef.
  • My Room: earn furniture and decorate the room where Momo and Bibi live.

More Momo games in different learning modes are on the way.
