## Records to keep (private, not in this repository)

For each song, keep the following in a safe place, so the licence can be shown if a store, platform or distributor asks:

1. The Suno song link or ID.
2. Proof that the Suno plan was **Pro or Premier on the day the song was made and downloaded**: the Suno account's plan or billing page, or the payment receipt for that month.
3. The original file as downloaded from Suno.

## Adding a new song

1. Make and download the song on a paid Suno plan.
2. Add the MP3 to `assets/audio/` with a short lowercase file name.
3. Add it to the `playlist` list in `assets/site-music.js`, in the order it should play.
4. Add a row to the table above, and keep the records listed above.
